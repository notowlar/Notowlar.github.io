How it began

I'm George, marketing and sales assistant at One Mortgage System. I found graphic design while studying games design at college and university: I kept ending up in Adobe and Canva making the artwork around the games, and by uni graphic design and 3D modelling had become my focus.

Now I design campaigns, magazine covers and motion pieces for OMS, and make posters and merch for myself on the side. I also regularly design the menus for a pop-up restaurant in Brighton and shoot their photography on the pop-up days. Outside of design I'm a huge car person, which is where pieces like the Volvo 340GL poster come from. I'm also building a modelling portfolio, so you'll find me on both sides of the camera here.
